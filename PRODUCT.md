# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective buyers evaluating the Vila Mascote apartment and licensed real-estate brokers preparing consistent listings or arranging qualified visits. Visitors need clear property facts, approved imagery, transparent costs, and a direct way to contact the owner.

## Product Purpose

Present the apartment accurately and confidently, help buyers decide whether to schedule a visit, and give brokers a reliable source of approved listing material. Success means fewer factual inconsistencies, qualified enquiries, and a straightforward path from evaluation to contact.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, and composed. The voice is direct and informative, with the polish of a well-maintained home and none of the pressure or exaggeration common in generic property listings.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered marketplace layouts, artificial scarcity, inflated luxury claims, generic stock imagery, and ambiguous commercial conditions. The site should not feel like a portal template or hide important facts behind lead-capture friction.

## Design Principles

- Lead with verified facts and real property photography.
- Keep the path from evaluation to owner contact obvious and low-friction.
- Use restrained visual polish to support credibility rather than spectacle.
- Preserve consistent source material for buyers, brokers, and third-party listings.
- Make important conditions readable before a visitor takes action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic HTML, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive alternative text, and reduced-motion accommodations. Key actions must remain understandable without relying on color or decorative imagery.
